Climate science

Agent-based modelling of food consumption and CO₂ emissions, University of Warsaw, 2024 – 2025.

Project

I developed and implemented an agent-based model in Python to simulate social imitation in the food-consumption behaviour of more than 80,000 European households, and the CO₂ emissions that follow from it. The work covered data preprocessing, social network generation and Monte Carlo experiments.

Paper

  • Soumik Dutta, Coralie Kerluc, Karolina Safarzyńska. Between-group interactions may delay a social tipping point in sustainable diets. 2026 · Submitted to Climatic Change
